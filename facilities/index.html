Facilities

Printmaking Studio

Our studio is equipped with 1 full sheet etching press (max print size 80 x 120cm), 1 litho press (max stone size 50 x 60cm), 1 4-color silkscreen carrousel (max frame size 30 x 40cm) and every tool and accessory needed to work on the techniques we offer.

Darkroom

Our darkroom is equipped with four enlargers that cover small (35mm), medium (6×6 & 6×7), and large (4×5) formats both for b&w and color. We also have a drum processors for developing films each dedicated to a specific process, C-41, E6 and B&W in all formats from 35mm to 8×10. Recently we added a RA-4 print processor for c-prints with the capacity of making prints of 50cm x 200cm.

Rental Fees

Darkroom

90$ / month (conditions apply). Does not include film chemistry, film and paper. Paper chemistry included.

Woodworking Workshop

Our woodworking workshop serves as a complement to the activities of our studio, such as making chassi, frames installations or any other needs.

The workshop is equipped with a table saw, band saw, miter saw, circular saw, router, jointer/thicknesser, bench drill, and all the hand tools you might need.

Other Services

Archival film processing & enlargements

Fees upon request

Our studio offers the service of film processing and enlargemetns for photographers and institutions seeking museum grade archivability.

This service is offered for black & white, color negative, and color positive (slide) upon demand. We can process 35mm, 120, 4×5 & 8×10 film sizes.

Our darkroom services are for those seeking professionalism in handling their material, achievability, and high-quality prints enlarged by hand.

WE DO NOT OFFER ANY DIGITAL SERVICES (and we are proud of that!).

Print Editioning

Fees upon request

We offer the service of pulling prints and editions for artists who work with lithography, intaglio, and relief printing.

Based on an artist’s proof, we can pull the edition to the specifications determined by the artist.

Like everything we do, this service is offered using archival materials.
